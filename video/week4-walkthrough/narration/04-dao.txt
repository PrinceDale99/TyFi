Now, governance. In the DAO tab, the farmer's voting power is read straight from their liquidity shares in the vault. A new proposal is open, and the farmer votes for it. The passkey signs the vote, and the tally updates on-chain. Quorum is thirty percent of all shares, and premium changes need a sixty percent supermajority. Earlier, the co-op passed proposal six: raise the Eastern Visayas premium multiplier to one hundred twenty-five percent. It then sat in a twelve-hour timelock, so members could react before anything changed.
